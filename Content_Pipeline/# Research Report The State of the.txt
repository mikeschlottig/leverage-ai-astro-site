Chief marketing officers (CMOs) are vital to driving organizational success by crafting go-to-market strategies, developing measurement frameworks, and aligning resources to achieve top-line goals. Every year, CMSWire surveys marketing leaders to understand their priorities, investment trends, and challenges.

In the latest_State of the CMO_report, we gathered insights from more than 500 marketing leaders across diverse industries and company sizes. This report provides a comprehensive look at the evolving role of CMOs and the skills necessary for success in today's competitive landscape.

**This report includes:**

*   10 key findings common across the marketing industry
*   Essential skills leadership considers critical for aspiring CMOs
*   Insights into how CMOs are handling the latest AI trends and tools

Download the report to stay ahead of marketing trends and drive success in your organization.
