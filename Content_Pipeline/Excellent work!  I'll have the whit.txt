The Money Flow — Avoiding Double Fees
This is the simplest part. You're operating as a reseller with net settlement. No fintech complexity needed.

How it works:

Customer → pays you (Stripe: ~2.9% + $0.30)  ← ONE fee, unavoidable
     ↓
You collect all orders
     ↓
Weekly: single ACH bank transfer to brother   ← FREE
(his cost × units shipped that week)
That's it. One Stripe fee, one free ACH. You keep the margin spread.

The ACH options:

Standard ACH (bank to bank): free, 1-3 business days. Use this.
Stripe Payouts to bank: $0.25 per payout if you want it from your Stripe balance, but just let it settle to your bank first
Zelle / Venmo Business: instant, free up to limits. Fine for early stage.
What you do NOT do:

Do not put brother on Stripe Connect — that's for marketplaces where third parties collect money independently. Stripe takes a cut there too.
Do not run his orders through your Stripe and then reverse-transfer — that's two events.
Logistics of the handoff:

Your Hono worker, after a successful Stripe webhook, does three things automatically:

Creates the order in D1
Emails brother a clean fulfillment slip (product name, his SKU, quantity, ship-to address)
Emails customer confirmation
Brother fulfills. When he ships, he replies to the email or hits a simple /api/orders/:id/ship endpoint you give him (password-protected, one form) that stores the tracking number and fires the customer a shipping email.

Weekly settlement: You look at total units fulfilled × his cost price, send one ACH. Done. No software needed for the money part — just a spreadsheet or your accounting tool.
