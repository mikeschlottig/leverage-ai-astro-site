## What is design strategy?

Design strategy is the discipline of creating visual systems that don't just look premium — they perform. Every color choice, typography decision, and layout pattern either builds authority with your audience or erodes it.

In an AI-first world, design strategy has a new dimension: your visual and structural choices directly affect how AI systems parse, trust, and cite your content. A well-designed page is also a well-structured page. I build both. One person. Full-stack. AI-multiplied. No account manager in the middle. You talk directly to the person building your infrastructure.

The distinction I care about most is between made content and decided content. Made content accumulates by accretion — each element added because it was available, because someone liked it, because a template included it. Decided content is built by a mind that actively chose every element over its alternatives. Design strategy is the process of making those choices explicit, consistent, and measurable. When every pixel has a reason for being where it is, the result is not just beautiful — it is legible to both humans and machines.

## How does design affect AI search visibility?

Most people think of design as purely visual. I think of it as structural. The same choices that make a page beautiful to a human eye — clear hierarchy, generous whitespace, intentional contrast — also make it parseable to an AI crawler. A page with logical heading structure, semantic HTML, and accessible markup is easier for a language model to understand than a page built from generic divs and inline styles.

I implement design systems that encode GEO best practices directly into the component layer. Every heading uses the correct semantic tag, not just styled text. Every image has descriptive alt text and structured caption markup. Every list is a real list, not a collection of styled divs. FAQ sections use proper schema-annotated HTML. These are design decisions that double as AI visibility infrastructure.

The relationship goes deeper than markup. AI systems are developing visual and layout analysis capabilities. They can infer page quality from structural signals: whether content is organized in scannable sections, whether key information appears above the fold, whether the page follows established patterns for its content type. A chaotic, accreted page signals low quality. A coherent, designed page signals authority. My design work ensures your pages send the right signals.

## What does a design engagement include?

My design engagements are anchored in research, not trends. I analyze your competitive landscape, your audience's visual literacy, and the context in which your brand needs to perform — then I build a system that serves all of it.

**Brand identity development** covers logo, color system, typography, iconography, and visual voice guidelines. I design for both human and machine audiences: the color palette must work in dark mode and light mode, the logo must be recognizable at favicon size, and the visual voice must be describable in text so AI systems can represent it accurately.

**UI/UX design** runs from wireframes through high-fidelity prototypes, built for conversion and accessibility. I design every screen state — empty, loading, error, success — because incomplete design systems break user trust. I also design for the AI-crawlable surface: every interactive element has a semantic fallback, and every dynamic state has a static representation that crawlers can index.

**Design system creation** produces component libraries, design tokens, and documentation your team can actually use. I build in code, not just Figma, because the design system is only real once it is deployable. Tokens are defined in CSS custom properties or a JS theme object. Components are built in Astro or React with explicit props and documented usage patterns.

**Web design implementation** delivers production-ready front-end code using Astro, React, and modern CSS architecture. I ship code that passes Core Web Vitals, renders correctly for AI crawlers, and degrades gracefully on older browsers. The implementation includes responsive breakpoints, dark mode support, and accessibility compliance.

**Content-design integration** ensures your design system supports the content architecture your GEO strategy requires. I work with your content — or create new content — to make sure the design and the words reinforce each other structurally and semantically.

## What platforms do you build on?

I primarily build on Astro, which is my recommended stack for AI-visible sites. Astro's islands architecture produces fast, lightweight pages with minimal JavaScript — exactly the kind of clean, parseable HTML that AI crawlers prefer. For interactive components, I use React islands with explicit client directives, so the page remains static and crawlable by default.

I also work with Webflow for clients who need a no-code CMS layer and want to manage content without touching code. Webflow's visual editor produces clean HTML when used correctly, and I can layer custom schema markup and GEO architecture on top of a Webflow foundation.

For complex applications — dashboards, internal tools, AI interfaces — I use React with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and modern state management. The platform choice always follows from the business requirements, not from my preferences. I recommend what will perform best for your specific situation.

## Do you work with existing brands?

Yes. I work in both brand-refresh mode and brand-build mode. In refresh mode, I audit your existing identity, identify what is working and what is undermining your goals, and evolve the system strategically. In build mode, I develop from scratch — but even then, I research your market and competitive landscape so the new identity is grounded in reality, not trend.

I'll recommend the appropriate scope after reviewing your current assets. Sometimes a brand needs a full rebuild because the existing identity is inconsistent, outdated, or technically broken. Sometimes it needs targeted refinement — a color system that works better in AI-surfaced contexts, a typography scale that improves readability, a component library that enforces semantic structure. I do not sell full rebuilds to brands that only need refinement.
